Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VidShare ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android application and website (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data — your username and a password hash used to sign in to the dashboard.
  • Uploaded content — the video files you upload, along with their name, size, type, and the share link we generate.
  • View statistics — anonymous play/view counts and basic timing used to show dashboard analytics.
  • Device & log data — app version, device model, and diagnostic logs that help us fix crashes.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data only to operate the Service: to let you sign in, store and stream your videos, generate share links, display your view statistics, and keep the app secure and improving. We do not sell your personal information.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data or run third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not read the contents of your videos beyond what is needed to process and play them.
  • We do not require access to contacts, messages, or your camera.

4. Sharing

Videos you upload are private by default and are only reachable through the unlisted share link you choose to distribute. We may share data with service providers (such as hosting) strictly to deliver the Service, or when required by law.

5. Retention & Deletion

We keep your uploaded videos and account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete a video or your account at any time from the dashboard; deletion removes the file and its associated share link.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures — such as authenticated access and encrypted transport — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the "Effective date" above will change accordingly. Continued use of the Service after updates means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at support@vidshare.app.
